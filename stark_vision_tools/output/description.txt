The rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence has sparked widespread concern about massive job losses, especially in white-collar professions. Experts like Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei predict AI could replace half of office-based roles within just a few years, potentially triggering unprecedented economic upheaval.

Yet history shows us a more optimistic path forward. Technological innovation—from mechanized farming to industrial automation—has consistently disrupted labor markets, only to create entirely new and valuable opportunities. Rather than AI replacing humans, it has the potential to greatly enhance our productivity and transform the nature of work itself. Imagine the possibilities when each employee doesn't just do their own work, but manages a team of AI agents, dramatically multiplying their value and output.

This transformation raises important questions: How can we leverage our uniquely human strengths—such as judgment, creativity, and taste—to thrive alongside AI? How does AI unlock previously uneconomical opportunities across different sectors, from personalized education to detailed business analysis and beyond? What skills will remain sought-after, and how can you develop AI fluency to remain indispensable in this changing landscape?

Join us in exploring how the Jevons Paradox suggests a future of increased possibilities rather than dwindling employment, and why adaptability, education, and forward-thinking policies are the keys to prosperity. Instead of worrying about AI taking jobs, let's consider how we can harness its full capabilities—and forge a future in which humans and AI together push the boundaries of what's possible.
